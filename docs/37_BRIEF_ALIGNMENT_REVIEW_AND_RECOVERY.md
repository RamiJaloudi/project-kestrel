## Product direction

[DESIGN] Keep the existing single API, PostgreSQL, authentication, centralized authorization, versioning, audit foundations, portal separation and source reader. Keep the user's requested administration, policy/playbook controls, service consoles and launchers as supporting capabilities. Center the Firm portal on the active matter and reviewers' work. Show clear engine/feature status and make incomplete features visibly future work rather than presenting them as usable deal tools.

[DESIGN] Separate taxonomy/playbook approval, batch processing authority, lawyer finding review, material rerun approval and audience publication. The brief requires an agreed taxonomy before broad AI use; it does not require an SME to manually launch every individual document. The existing per-document initial-SME authorization adds friction. Review its replacement by scoped batch authority against prior user-approved governance before changing it. Preserve independent SME authorization for material historical reruns and independent publication decisions.

## Recovery build order and acceptance

1. **Board-focused review workspace.** Implement matter overview, coverage and customer-contract register; integrated source/evidence review with correct/confirm/reject/manual finding and follow-up. A lawyer must be able to find a priority contract, inspect its citation, correct the result and update the register without navigating technical administration. Use richer synthetic fixtures with negative cases, German licences, missing documents/scans and changed versions.
2. **Reviewed findings into report and client collaboration.** Generate editable draft report sections and a board-priority consent annex, approve a complete audience version, open the correct Halden/Castlegate view, and record client comments/questions. Prove bank privilege denial and source-linked correction/republication behavior.
3. **Close confidentiality and processing gaps.** Named Halden clean-team workflow, scoped finding-access audit, controlled client uploads, configured OCR and representative AI evaluation. Shared policy must cover every new surface. Live demo processing stays within the approved $2/run and $10/month limits; enabling an adapter must not override restrictive policy.
4. **Complete remaining core requirements.** Permission-aware document Q&A and seller-Q&A tracking; signing freeze and deletion/retention/hold workflow; durable impact tasks and authorized reruns. Complete deployment/recovery/scale design and evidence for the assessment.

Submission work runs alongside these increments: plain-language partner note, conflicts/decision owners, day-one existing-tools plan, architecture/data flow, quality/cost/operations assumptions, rollout and AI disclosure. Demonstrate a coherent user journey, with actual limitations declared. Public deployment remains a separate user-approved gate.
